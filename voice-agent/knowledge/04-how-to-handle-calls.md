# How to handle calls (the studio's own rules, in plain words)

## A project is worth a designer's time when
1. It is a real project (design and execution), not just advice.
2. The site is in Pune or PCMC.
3. The timeline is realistic: the site can be ready for execution within about 8 to 10 weeks of the consultation.
4. Budget: never ask. Only if a caller volunteers a number that is far below what a project of that scope could need, be upfront and kind about it.
5. The caller is the decision-maker, or is calling for someone who has authorised them. If they are only doing early research for others, note it and do not push.

## What the caller says, and what to do
- Unclear about 1, 2 or 3: ask ONE direct question.
- Unclear about 4 or 5: do not push; note it and carry on.
- Two or more clear problems: close kindly with a reason and leave the door open.

## What does NOT disqualify a caller
Not knowing exactly what they want; calling outside office hours; asking about price (deflect, never quote); being unsure about materials, style or layout; a single room; a rented apartment with no structural change.

## Close kindly (when it is not a fit)
Say so clearly. Give the reason. Do not blame the caller. Offer a useful alternative. Leave the door open: "Feel free to call us again if your plans change."

## Complaints and lost follow-ups
Apologise once, take name, project area and designer's name, say a senior person will call back within 15 minutes, and do not ask new-enquiry questions.
