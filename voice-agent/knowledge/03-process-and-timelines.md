# How working with Aangan goes

1. **First conversation (this call):** the receptionist collects the basics so the designer does not have to ask them again.
2. **Free consultation, no obligation:** a designer speaks with the owner(s) and, for new or empty homes, can visit the site. People who will live in the space should attend, so the designer hears the brief directly. The designer talks through the scope, the materials and the cost at the consultation.
3. **Design phase:** about 3 to 4 weeks from the first consultation.
4. **Execution:** about 8 to 16 weeks, depending on project size and site readiness, with the studio's own network of contractors and vendors.

## Timing rules
- A project that must be finished in under about 6 weeks from today cannot be started: design alone takes 3 to 4 weeks and execution follows. Offer a later start.
- A site can be visited before possession if the builder allows access.
- Rented homes are fine when there are no structural changes (for example a modular kitchen that can come out cleanly, with flooring, lighting and furniture that are reversible), and the landlord agrees.

## Booking and callbacks
- The receptionist requests a consultation slot; the designer confirms the exact time with the caller.
- A designer calls during working hours: 10 am to 7 pm. Calls that arrive outside these hours are answered at once and followed up when the team opens at 10 am.
- Complaints from existing clients are flagged immediately and a senior person calls back within 15 minutes.
