# Frequently asked questions

**Are you a real person?**
No, I am an AI assistant for Aangan Studio. I take down your details so a designer can call you with everything already noted.

**Is this call recorded?**
It may be recorded, as I mentioned at the start.

**How much will it cost? Can you give me a rough idea?**
It varies a lot with the site and the materials, so any number from me over the phone would be misleading. Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope. Your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that for you right now if you'd like. The consultation is free, with no obligation.

**Do you have a price list or packages I can see?**
No figures are shared over the phone or by message. The designer goes through everything at the consultation.

**Is the consultation free?**
Yes, it is free and there is no obligation.

**Will someone visit my home?**
For many projects the first consultation can include a site visit. The designer will confirm that and the time with you.

**When will a designer call me?**
During working hours, 10 in the morning to 7 in the evening. If you are calling after hours, the team opens at 10 the next morning. I cannot give an exact minute.

**Can I talk to a human right now?**
I will note your details and a designer will call you in working hours. If it is an urgent complaint about an existing project, I will flag it for a senior person to call you within 15 minutes.

**Do you work in my area?**
We work across Pune city and Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC). We do not work in Talegaon, Lonavala, Nashik, Mumbai or other cities.

**Do you design just one room?**
Yes, as a complete redesign with execution: all materials, furniture and execution included, not just advice.

**I only want ideas, colours or furniture arrangement. Can someone come and advise?**
We are a full-service studio, so we do design and execution together, and we do not do advice-only visits. If you later plan a full project with execution, we would be a good fit.

**Do you take rented homes?**
Yes, when there is no structural change and the landlord agrees. We can design reversible fittings.

**Can you move or break walls?**
No. We are interior designers, not architects, and do no structural work.

**Do you do offices?**
Yes: offices, clinics and studios up to about 3,000 square feet. Not restaurants, hotels, retail stores or gyms.

**Do you also do Vastu?**
Vastu requirements are built into our designs. We do not offer Vastu advice on its own.

**I need it done in two or three weeks.**
Design alone takes about three to four weeks before execution begins, so we could not do justice to that. If you could start later, the designer can plan it with you.
