# Service area

Aangan Studio serves **Pune city and PCMC (Pimpri-Chinchwad)**.

## Pune city (examples; adjoining areas are fine)
Kothrud, Baner, Aundh, Wakad, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar, Magarpatta, NIBM, Kondhwa, Undri, Shivane, Warje, Erandwane, Deccan, and adjoining areas such as Kharadi, Bavdhan, Pashan, Balewadi, Wanowrie and Nanded City.

## PCMC
Pimpri, Chinchwad, Pimple Saudagar, Pimple Nilakh, Ravet, Hinjewadi, Pimple Gurav, Akurdi.

## Not served at the moment
Talegaon, Lonavala, Nashik, Mumbai and other cities. The studio's contractor and vendor network is in Pune, so execution outside it would not be of the quality the studio wants to promise.

If a locality is not on these lists, ask the caller which part of Pune it is near. If it is clearly outside Pune and PCMC, explain kindly (see the call handling file).
