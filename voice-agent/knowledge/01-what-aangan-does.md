# What Aangan Studio does

Aangan Studio is an interior design studio in Pune offering end-to-end interior design and execution for homes and small commercial spaces across Pune and PCMC.

## Services
- Space planning and layout optimisation
- Material selection (flooring, wall finishes, ceilings)
- Furniture design and curation (custom and sourced)
- Lighting design and specification
- Kitchen and wardrobe design
- Execution supervision, using the studio's own network of contractors and vendors
- Vastu requirements are built into designs

## Residential projects
Apartments, independent houses and villas: a full home, specific floors, or individual rooms, where the scope is a complete redesign with execution.
- Full home: 2BHK onwards, design and execution.
- Partial home: a full floor, or two or more rooms with execution.
- Single room: a bedroom or living room, as a complete redesign with all materials, furniture and execution. Not just advice.

## Commercial projects
Offices, clinics and studios up to about 3,000 square feet: workstations, cabins, reception and common areas. Retail and hospitality (restaurants, hotels) are not taken on as primary projects.

## What Aangan does not do
- Architecture and structural work: no moving walls, no changes to the building structure, no permits for structural changes. The studio does interiors, not architecture.
- Decor and styling advice only (colours, rearranging furniture): the minimum engagement is a room redesign with execution.
- Standalone furniture sourcing without a design project.
- Vastu consultation only (Vastu is included in designs, not offered alone).
- Restaurants, hotels, retail stores and gyms.
- Projects outside Pune and PCMC.
